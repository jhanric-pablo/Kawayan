## 1. Opening frame (functionality focus)

### One sentence

> "Kawayan AI is a **functional prototype** for Filipino MSMEs — one platform where a small business owner can **register, get verified, set up their brand, plan and create social content, schedule posts, pay through a local wallet, track growth, and get support** — all in one workflow."

### Universal answer (when blank or question is broad)

> "Sa proposal stage po, ang ipinapakita namin ay functional prototype — hindi pa commercial product, pero real modules na naka-wire end-to-end. Ang main functionality ng Kawayan ay tumulong sa Filipino MSME na mag-plan, gumawa, mag-schedule, mag-track, magbayad, at humingi ng support sa isang platform — with human review before posting. Ang specific na tanong po ay [repeat their question] — sa aming design, [give one concrete example: calendar / verification / wallet / support]. Ang full production hardening at pilot testing ay bahagi ng implementation phase."

### Proposal-stage honesty (say once at start)

> "At proposal stage, we are not presenting a finished commercial product. We are presenting a **high-fidelity functional prototype** that proves our design works end-to-end, while full production deployment, hardening, and scale testing remain part of our implementation phase."

## 2. End-to-end flow (memorize)

```
Register + TOS → Upload business docs → Admin verifies
    → Brand DNA survey → Content calendar
    → AI plans month → Generate captions + images
    → Owner edits → Schedule → Post (extension assist)
    → Growth Insights → Wallet/Billing if needed → Support if stuck
```

**Tech stack (one breath):** React + Vite frontend, Node/Express API, SQLite database, JWT auth, Socket.io for real-time support, AI via cloud/local LLM, Xendit for payments.

## 4. Module-by-module functionality

### Registration & verification
- Sign up, accept **Terms of Service**
- Upload business proof → status **Pending** until admin approves
- **Why:** Trust layer — only real MSMEs get full access

### Brand DNA
- Survey: business name, industry, audience, brand voice, themes
- **Function:** Personalizes AI output (prompt-based, not fine-tuned per user)

### Content calendar (main feature)
- Default screen after login; whole month at a glance
- Edit caption, change image, regenerate (max 2×), upload photo, Save Draft, Schedule
- **Human-in-the-loop:** AI suggests; owner approves — **no silent auto-posting**

### AI content (keep short unless they ask)
- Plan Month → ideas; Batch Create → captions + images
- Virality score = AI estimate only, not guarantee
- Fallback templates if API fails

### Scheduling & posting
- Statuses: **Draft → Scheduled → Published**
- Chrome **extension assists** posting — user still controls final post
- **Honest:** "Scheduling workflow is in the app; full social API auto-posting is partial at prototype stage."

### Billing & wallet
- Prepaid wallet; top-up via **Xendit** (sandbox in demo)
- Pro subscription unlocks 16 posts; **₱150 add-on** beyond monthly cap

### Growth Insights
- Engagement per platform, charts, simple ROI
- Data via **browser extension** sync
- **Honest:** "Workflow works; official Meta/TikTok APIs expandable in implementation."

### Support
- MSME: widget — AI chat, tickets, call request
- Support agent: real-time ticket dashboard (`support@kawayan.ph`)
- Categories: Technical, Billing, etc.

### Admin
- Verify business documents; approve pending wallet payments
- Manage users, subscriptions, platform growth/retention stats

## 5. Roles & likely functionality questions

### Three roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **MSME User** | Brand setup, calendar, AI content, billing, insights, support widget |
| **Support Agent** | Tickets, calls, billing-related support tools |
| **Administrator** | Verify businesses, approve payments, manage users, platform oversight |

**Security:** JWT login, bcrypt passwords, role-based access.

### Q: "Ano ang main functionality ng system?"

> "Isang platform para sa Filipino MSME: **plan at gumawa ng social media content**, **i-schedule sa calendar**, **magbayad sa local wallet**, **tingnan ang performance sa Growth Insights**, at **kumuha ng tulong sa integrated support** — lahat after business verification."

### Q: "Gumagana ba lahat ng features?"

> "Ang **core user journey** gumagana sa demo environment: login, verification gating, brand setup, calendar, content generation, scheduling, wallet, insights, at support. Ang ilan — tulad ng **live Xendit payments** at **full social API posting** — nasa **sandbox o extension-assisted** mode sa prototype. Documented namin sa feature checklist."

### Q: "Paano niyo sine-test ang functionality?"

> "Manual end-to-end testing sa dev environment gamit seeded accounts — MSME, support, admin. May **feature checklist** na naka-map sa objectives. Quantitative evaluation with real MSME users ay sa pilot phase."

### Q: "Ano ang pinagkaiba niyo sa Canva, ChatGPT, o Hootsuite?"

> "Hindi kami single-purpose tool. **Pinagsama namin** ang Philippine context sa isang workflow: MSME verification, Taglish AI na personalized sa Brand DNA, editorial calendar, **GCash/Maya-style wallet**, growth analytics, at local support desk — para sa owner na hindi marketing expert."

### Q: "Paano ang scheduling — automatic ba ang pag-post?"

> "**Hindi automatic posting** without user action. User nagre-review, nag-s-schedule, tapos **browser extension** ang tumutulong sa actual post. Human-in-the-loop by design."

### Q: "Prototype lang ba o tapos na?"

> "**Functional prototype** po sa proposal stage. Real modules, real database, real flows — pero hindi pa commercially deployed. Implementation phase: cloud hosting, pilot MSMEs, production payment, full API integrations."

## 7. "Bakit pare-pareho / hindi accurate ang AI?"

### Do NOT say

- "Wala kaming ginamit na AI" — may LLM pipeline kayo  
- "Prototype lang kaya okay lang mali" — sounds lazy  
- "Same lang naman lahat ng AI"

### Core frame (memorize)

> "At proposal stage, we validated the **AI workflow** — not final content quality. We use **prompt-based personalization** from Brand DNA, not custom-trained models per business. Repetition or inaccuracy can happen with free/low-cost LLMs, especially in batch generation. That is why **human-in-the-loop** is built in: the owner edits, regenerates, or uploads their own content before scheduling. Production phase = better models, prompt tuning, and pilot feedback from real MSMEs."

### Q: "Bakit pare-pareho ang generated content?"

> "Possible po sa prototype dahil: (1) **same Brand DNA inputs** produce similar prompt context, (2) **batch generation** — smaller models repeat patterns, (3) kung nag-fail ang API, **fallback templates** ang lumalabas. Hindi pa kami nag-**fine-tune per user** — personalization is **prompt-based** lang mula sa Brand Survey."

> "Ang system ay **drafting assistant**, hindi final publisher. Kahit mag-repeat ang AI, ang owner **nag-e-edit, nag-re-regenerate (max 2), o nag-u-upload ng sariling photo** bago i-schedule."

### Q: "Hindi accurate ang Taglish / caption"

> "Tama po — **LLM output is not 100% accurate**, lalo na sa Taglish. Sa prototype, gumamit kami ng **accessible LLM** (Unsloth / optional Ollama) instead of paid enterprise models — partly **capstone budget and API quota limits**; maraming calls per user per month ang system.
>
> Accuracy layers: **(1) Brand DNA sa prompt**, **(2) JSON validation**, **(3) user review before publish**. Hindi namin ina-claim na perfect ang AI; ina-claim namin na **may working workflow** where the MSME stays in control."

### Q: "So ano ang point kung mali naman?"

> "Ang point ay **system functionality**, hindi perfect copywriting. ChatGPT at Canva also need editing. Ang difference: **integrated MSME workflow** — calendar, tiers, wallet, insights, support — at **Philippine context**. AI quality improvement ay **implementation deliverable**: pilot, measure **edit rate**, refine prompts or switch to stronger paid model."

### Q: "Bakit hindi Gemini / better AI?"

> "Planned po ang Gemini sa design, pero sa prototype **hindi primary** dahil sa **free-tier quota** — isang Batch Create, maraming LLM calls. Pinili namin ang **free/low-cost stack** para ma-demo nang paulit-ulit. Architecture **provider-agnostic** — swap sa backend, hindi rebuild ng UI. Quality upgrade = implementation phase."

### Demo playsafe (output looks same live)

> "Expected at prototype with batch / fallback / same demo account. Ang ipapakita namin ay **workflow**: Brand DNA → generate → **edit** → schedule. Sa pilot, tune prompts per industry at track edit rate."

Then **edit one word** on screen.

### One-liner lusot

> "Hindi namin ina-claim na perfect ang AI sa prototype — ina-claim namin na gumagana ang system: AI drafts, MSME reviews, then schedules. Repetition and inaccuracy are known LLM limitations; human review now + better models in implementation are the answer."

### Why it looks same (technical)

| Cause | Explanation |
|-------|-------------|
| Same demo account | `cafe@kawayan.ph` — same Brand DNA every time |
| Batch mode | Many posts in one run → repeated structures |
| Free/smaller LLM | Less variety than paid GPT-4 / Gemini |
| Fallback templates | Generic Taglish when API fails |
| No fine-tuning | Prompt context only, not per-business training |

### Tagalog version

> "Sa prototype po, hindi pa namin ino-optimize ang quality ng bawat caption — pinatunayan muna namin na kaya ng system ang buong flow: mag-plan, mag-generate, mag-edit, mag-schedule. Pare-pareho minsan dahil prompt-based lang ang personalization at free/low-cost ang model. Kaya mandatory ang review ng owner bago i-post. Sa implementation: prompt tuning, pilot sa tunay na MSME, at pwedeng lumipat sa mas malakas na paid LLM."

## 8. "You said it's a high-fidelity prototype"

### Define it in one sentence

> "**High-fidelity functional prototype** means real working modules — real login, database, and end-to-end flows — that look and behave like the intended product, but it is **not yet** commercially deployed, load-tested, or production-hardened."

### Four levels of prototype

| Level | What it is | Your project |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| **Low-fi** | Wireframes, slides, mockups only | You went **beyond** |
| **Mid-fi** | Clickable UI, fake data | You went **beyond** |
| **High-fi functional prototype** | Real code, real DB, core flows work in demo | **This is you** |
| **Production system** | Cloud, scaled, live payments, audited, pilot users | **Not yet** |

**Key line:**

> "High-fidelity refers to fidelity to the **design and workflow**, not fidelity to a **finished commercial launch**."

### Q: "Prototype lang — bakit high-fidelity?"

> "Prototype po sa **stage** — proposal validation. High-fidelity po sa **implementation depth**: hindi Figma lang. May real authentication, verification gating, content calendar, wallet, insights, support tickets, admin panel — naka-connect sa backend at database."

### Q: "Mukhang tapos na — product na ba?"

> "Understandable po — ginawa namin ang UI at core modules nang maaga para ma-prove ang feasibility. Pero hindi pa commercial product: **localhost**, **Xendit sandbox**, **extension-assisted analytics**, walang real MSME pilot at scale testing. Goal sa proposal: **prove the design works**; productization sa implementation."

### Prototype vs final system

| Area | High-fi prototype | Final system |
|------|-------------------|--------------|
| Hosting | Local dev | Cloud, domain, SSL |
| Users | Seeded demo accounts | Real MSME pilots |
| Payments | Xendit sandbox | Live merchant + compliance |
| AI quality | Accessible LLM, prompts | Tuned / paid models, pilot feedback |
| Social | Calendar + extension assist | Full official APIs where allowed |
| Security | JWT, roles, bcrypt | Security audit, pen test |
| Stability | Demo environment | Uptime, monitoring, backups |

**One-liner:** "Same design intent; different maturity level."

### Q: "Bakit hindi wireframe lang sa proposal?"

> "Pwede po wireframe, pero mas mataas ang risk — baka hindi feasible ang AI + payments + support sa isang app. Kaya high-fidelity functional prototype: **reduce technical risk early** at maipakita na objectives may **working feature mapping**."

### Q: "Ano exactly ang high-fidelity sa inyo?" (5 proofs)

1. Real auth — register, login, JWT, roles  
2. Real data — SQLite, not mock-only screens  
3. Real workflows — verify → Brand DNA → calendar → schedule → wallet → ticket  
4. Real integrations (prototype mode) — LLM proxy, Xendit sandbox, Socket.io  
5. Real UI — production-intent dashboard  

**Not high-fidelity yet:** operations at scale — deployment, live billing, enterprise security, pilot evaluation.

### Q: "Misleading ba na sabihing high-fidelity?"

> "Hindi po kung clear ang definition. Sinabi namin **functional prototype** — hindi **finished product**. High-fidelity = how closely the prototype matches **intended system behavior**, not that we are on the App Store or serving paying merchants at national scale."

### Emergency short answers

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Define high-fidelity | Real code, real database, real end-to-end flows — not mockups. |
| Prototype or product? | Prototype for proposal feasibility; productization is next phase. |
| Why build so much for proposal? | Prove integration risk is manageable before final implementation. |

### Closing statement (memorize)

> "In summary, Kawayan AI at proposal stage is a **high-fidelity functional prototype** that demonstrates our intended MSME platform: verified onboarding, AI-assisted content calendar, local payments, analytics, and integrated support. We built enough working features to prove technical and design feasibility. Our next phase is deployment, pilot testing with real MSMEs, and production hardening. Thank you."
