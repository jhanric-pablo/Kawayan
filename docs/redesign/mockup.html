Questions

Still stuck? Open a help ticket after you sign up and someone from our team will answer.

Do I need to be good at social media?

No. Answer a few questions about your shop and Kawayan writes the captions, makes the images and picks good times. You just approve.

Can I edit what the AI writes?

Yes. Edit any caption, regenerate it up to two times, or upload your own photo instead of the generated image.

Which platforms does it post to?

Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts. You connect them once in Settings.

What happens when I run out of posts?

Your drafts stay safe. Buy one extra post for ₱150, upgrade to Pro, or wait for your quota to reset on the 1st.

Why do you need my business permit?

To keep Kawayan for real businesses. Your permit is stored privately and only our review team can open it.
